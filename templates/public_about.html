About EduCertify

EduCertify is an online learning and certification platform built to help students gain real, verifiable skills through structured courses, hands-on lessons, and assessments. Instructors design courses with modules, lessons, and quizzes, while our verification system ensures every certificate issued can be checked for authenticity — instantly, without needing an account.

Structured Learning

Real Assessments

Quizzes and final assessments that verify actual understanding.

Verifiable Certificates

Every certificate has a unique ID and QR code anyone can verify.
