ABOUT EDUCERTIFY

Learning should lead somewhere.

EduCertify is a learning and certification platform created to make skill development more structured, practical and meaningful for students.

Learn
Assess
Certify
OUR APPROACH

More than just watching lessons.

EduCertify is designed around an idea that learning is more useful when students can actually demonstrate what they have learned.

Courses are organized into modules and lessons. Students track their progress, complete quizzes and assessments, and can eventually earn certificates based on their completed learning requirements.

Structured courses and lessons
Progress tracking
Knowledge assessments
Public certificate verification
HOW IT WORKS

From first lesson to certification.

A straightforward learning flow designed for students.

01

Choose a course

Find a course that matches your interests, goals or career direction.

02

Learn and practice

Work through lessons and complete the learning activities provided by instructors.

03

Complete assessments

Test your understanding through quizzes and required assessments.

04

Earn your certificate

Meet the requirements and receive a certificate that can be publicly verified.
